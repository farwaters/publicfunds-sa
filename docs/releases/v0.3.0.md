# Thusa / PublicFunds SA v0.3.0: release notes (DRAFT)

**Date:** 19 Oct 2026 · **Change:** CHG-2026-006 (Normal-high) · **ADR:** ADR-0010 · **Branch:** `feat/public-dashboard`
· **Status:** awaiting review. Not published.

## What's new
- **Public dashboard.** A no-login website for taxpayers, published with Grok Build at a grok.me address.
  - It is one static file, `apps/public-dashboard/index.html`, with no backend, cookies or trackers.
  - Mobile-first and accessible: skip link, visible focus, labelled controls, tables behind every chart, and
    symbols as well as colour.
  - Pages:
    - home (headline numbers, freshness per source, "What we don't know");
    - coverage heatmap (data type, province, type, search);
    - municipality pages (budget vs actual, irregular / fruitless & wasteful / unauthorised trend, audit opinion
      history with report links, tenders by year, award records);
    - national departments;
    - tenders;
    - confirmed flags;
    - sources & method.
  - Tools: CPI inflation adjuster, fuel price checker, compare two municipalities, and per resident (unknown until
    population data exists).
  - Every table can be downloaded as CSV.
  - To publish it, paste `apps/public-dashboard/GROK_BUILD_PROMPT.md` into grok.com Build mode.
- **Public data export: `pf publish-data` writes `public-data/v1/`.**
  - Versioned JSON (`publicfunds.public-data/v1`), served from GitHub raw once merged.
  - Contents:
    - manifest (build time in SAST, sources with licence and status, checksums, counts, known gaps and errors);
    - coverage;
    - municipalities;
    - national departments;
    - tender summaries by buyer and year (missing-value share, supplier concentration without names);
    - award records per year;
    - confirmed flags plus hidden-lead counts;
    - fuel prices and CPI.
  - Money is in cents, `null` means unknown, and every figure has a source.
  - Size about 4.3 MB in total, with a budget of 5 MB in total and 2 MB per file.
  - The export **refuses** in two cases: a failed data-quality gate, or a safety-scan hit (reviewer data, notes,
    tokens, SA ID numbers, secret-shaped values, restricted-person names).
- **Price benchmarks:**
  - DMPR fuel prices: 7 grades, Jan–Oct 2026, from the monthly "History of Fuel Prices" table.
  - CPI for South Africa from OECD SDMX (CC BY 4.0), monthly 2008-01 to 2025-01. Stats SA itself blocks scripted
    downloads (KE-003).
  - New tables `curated.fuel_price` and `curated.cpi`, and 3 DQ checks.
- **Refresh flow:**
  - `scripts/publish-public-data.sh` commits only `public-data/`. With `--push`, it fast-forwards `main`.
  - The `publicfunds-refresh` skill runs it after a successful refresh.
  - The push is pre-authorised only from a routine the owner approves. No routine has been created.

## Known limitations
- No confirmed flags yet (0). 335 automated leads are hidden pending review, and only their counts are public.
- Population is unknown, so per-resident figures show "unknown" (CSI-024).
- CPI ends 2025-01 because the OECD series lags (KE-012, CSI-023). Fuel prices cover 2026 only (KE-012, CSI-022).
- Tender award values are as published. Many are missing (KE-007), and some are multi-year or framework values.
- GitHub raw URLs work only after merge to `main`, and are cached for about 5 minutes.

## Upgrade / deploy
1. Merge the PR.
2. On the bot box: `git pull && uv run thusa run && scripts/publish-public-data.sh`. This adds the price tables to
   the main warehouse.
3. Publish with Grok Build (D-16). Optionally add a custom domain (D-17).
4. Optionally, approve a daily routine for the `publicfunds-refresh` skill.

## Tests
- `pytest`: the full suite passes. It includes 10 new export tests: no unconfirmed-flag leak, no PII, schema and
  checksums, cents or null, size budget, quality gate, and the price parsers.
- `scripts/dashboard_check.py`: 11 pages × desktop/mobile, no console errors or failed requests, and the
  interactive tools work.
