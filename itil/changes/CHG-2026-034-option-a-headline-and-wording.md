## Problem
The headline "75.7% complete" (1167 of 1542 municipality-years, FYE 2020-2025, 2019/20 to 2024/25) counts 2024/25 as a gap for every municipality. In the data PublicFunds loads today there is nothing for 2024/25 UIFW and audit opinion for any municipality, and for budget 256 of 257 have the original but not the adjusted budget. A reader of the home page, the coverage page or a report card cannot tell a year nobody has from a year one municipality lacks. CHG-2026-033 says this on the "By data type" table only. Separately, a few state labels still say "published" or "not published", which is a claim about the source that the data does not support (KE-006 marks the cause "[unverified why]"; AGSA's 2024-25 annexures exist as PDFs, so "not published" would be wrong for them).

## The choice: option A (delegated), and the rejected alternatives with their numbers
**A (chosen).** Keep the headline as it is, 75.7% = 1167/1542 over 2019/20 to 2024/25. Show 2024/25 separately and flag it: one line next to the headline and on the coverage page and report card. The 2024/25 gap stays counted in every percentage and stays visible as a gap, so the headline does not rise because a source is late.
**Rejected alternatives** (not built; numbers recomputed on 2026-10-24 from the committed `public-data/v1`, an independent recount from the per-entity state strings that reproduces `headline` and `headline.by_type` exactly):
- **B, window ends at the last year every source has reached (FYE 2020-2024):** headline 1167/1285 = **90.8%**.
- **C1, ignore whole types with nothing loaded for any municipality (UIFW, audit opinion) in 2024/25:** headline 1167/1542 = 75.7% (budget 2024/25 is still partial for 256 of 257), UIFW 1285/1285 and audit opinion 1238/1285 for five years.
- **C2, also ignore budget cells that lack only the adjusted budget:** headline 1412/1542 = **91.6%** (1413/1542 = 91.6% if all 257 budget cells are ignored).

Details of the drafts (province order under B, ranking effect, guards) are in the unpushed revision 3 and are not repeated here.

1. A wrong line (a gap labelled "no municipality has it" that one municipality in fact has) would hide a gap. Mitigation: the line comes from the same rule as CHG-2026-033's banner (a type-year is empty when no municipality has a fully present cell), tested against a Python reference on the real data and on seven mutated data sets.
2. The note is bold text of two lines on a phone; layout was checked at 390 px and 1366 px, light and dark.
3. After 1 December 2026 the headline falls (prediction above) and the note gains a line. That is the intended effect of A (the gap stays visible), not a defect, but it will look like a drop.
4. The home note is now four lines on a phone (two context lines added in revision 5). Layout was checked at 390 px and 1366 px, light and dark. The prediction line carries a date and the words "Prediction, not a result"; it is wrong if FYE 2026 is not like FYE 2025, which is why it says "if".
