## Problem

## Change
1. **Source and licence.** New source type, recorded in `config/sources.yaml` (`dmpr_fuel.history_xlsx`) and in the connector comment:
   *DMPR fuel regulations xlsx (government public information)*. DMPR Terms & Conditions (https://www.dmpr.gov.za/Terms-Conditions, read 2026-10-24), Copyright Notice: the material may be reproduced free of charge in any format or media without permission, **provided that** it is reproduced accurately; it is not used in a misleading or derogatory manner; it is not used for any financial or commercial gain; and the source, including the website URL, and copyright status are clearly acknowledged.
   How each condition is met: accurate - figures are copied as published, unrounded, and cross-checked against the November 2025 history table (7/7 equal); not misleading - one-line grade/zone labels as in CHG-2026-020/021/023, and the "unknown" and "from the workbook" wording follow-up below; not for financial or commercial gain - as at 2026-10-24 the dashboard carries no advertising, tracking or charge (checked in the repo and the live site); the owner is to confirm there is no cost-recovery or monetisation; source and copyright - the source URL is carried in every row's lineage (`curated.fuel_price.source_url`, manifest `sources`), and the public licence line (follow-up below) names DMPR as the copyright holder.
2. **Connector** (`prices.py`): `run_fuel_regulations_xlsx()` called from `run_fuel` after the history fetch. Fetch once (`store.latest` check); the body must start with `PK`;
   a 404, block or non-workbook is noted in the run result, never stored, never fatal. Only the gap month is listed (`FUEL_REGULATIONS_XLSX`), so the other months stay with the history PDFs
   (the curated model keeps the most recently fetched file per month and grade; a November workbook in the `dmpr_fuel` raw store would have silently replaced the PDF rows, so none is stored there).
3. **Parser** `fuel_regulations_xlsx_rows(blob, name)`: stdlib zip/xml reader (`xlsx_sheet`, the AGSA reader generalised to a named sheet; `xlsx_first_sheet` unchanged in behaviour). **Cells only**; `docProps/*` is never opened or read (test plants an unparseable `docProps/core.xml`).
   Fails closed with `ValueError` (no partial rows) if: a worksheet is missing; a section heading is absent or appears twice; a section has no or several "EFFECTIVE d MONTH yyyy" cells;
   sections disagree on year/month or the file name's month/year differs from the workbook; the ZONES header row or the price column header (ACTUAL / RTL) is missing; a zone row (9C or 1A) is missing or found twice;
   a price cell is non-numeric, blank, or outside 500-10000 c/l. Emits the 7 grades with `source_locator` `December-2025-Fuel-Regulations.xlsx#Petrol!9C@K45` form.
   The user's example `#Petrol!9C` is not unique (three petrol sections), so the cell is appended after `@`. Staging prefixes the file name, as it does for the PDFs.
4. **Staging** (`staging.py`): `.xlsx` files use the new parser. No SQL, schema, DQ or manifest-code change: `curated.fuel_price` and the DQ gate (`unique:fuel_price`, `fuel_price_plausible`) take the rows as they are.
5. **Cross-check script** `scripts/fuel_xlsx_crosscheck.py` (`--fetch` downloads Jan-Nov 2025 workbooks into git-ignored `data/xcheck/`, outside the raw store and warehouse; compares with the stored 2025 history PDF; exits 1 on a November mismatch).
