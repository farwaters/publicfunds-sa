## Problem
After #24 the live text says "One month is missing: December 2025 ... so it shows as unknown" (`known_gaps` KE-012, KE-012 row, and by omission `prices.fuel.about`: "Only months published in the loaded DMPR history table are included"). The text would be false in two ways: a month said to be missing is present, and the source is not only the history table.
