## Why KE-015
Open KE numbers checked 2026-10-24 on origin/main (highest KE-013) and on every remote branch: the highest anywhere is KE-014 (PR #22, `chg-2026-024-diesel-oct2022-note`); no branch uses KE-015. PR #24 (`chg-2026-026-known-gaps-ke012`) edits the KE-012 row only.

## Problem
The DMPR history PDF and DMPR's own fuel-regulations workbooks disagree on the coast 95 petrol figure in two months, January 2025 and March 2024. The history table is what the dashboard publishes
(`curated.fuel_price`, grade `petrol_95_coast`), so a reader of the live page sees two figures that DMPR's own workbooks contradict, with no note saying so. The January 2025 case was found in CHG-2026-027;
the March 2024 case was added on Scout's report and checked here.

- Freight News was named by Scout; I did not open it and do not cite it. BusinessTech's table (coastal 95 R23.73, 93 R23.41) appeared in a search result but its page timed out when fetched, so it is not cited.

  The same table lists **2028** for 1 Jan 2025 coastal 95, a third figure that matches neither the PDF (2055) nor the xlsx (2080); it is probably its own typo, not relied on and not adjudicated.

Conclusion: in both months a probable slip in DMPR's PDF (the 93 coast figure in the 95 coast column). **Cause unverified; DMPR has not been asked.**
