## Why KE-015
Open KE numbers checked 2026-10-24 on origin/main (highest KE-013) and on every remote branch: the highest anywhere is KE-014 (PR #22, `chg-2026-024-diesel-oct2022-note`); no branch uses KE-015. PR #24 (`chg-2026-026-known-gaps-ke012`) edits the KE-012 row only.

## Problem
The DMPR history PDF and DMPR's own fuel-regulations workbooks disagree on the **coast 95 petrol** figure in **five months** (2020-05, 2021-01, 2021-04, 2024-03, 2025-01; re-measured 2026-10-24, see "Other PDF-versus-xlsx differences"). Only two of them, March 2024 and January 2025, show one pattern:
the PDF's coast 95 figure equals the xlsx's coast 93 figure. The other three differ in another way (2020-05: 1252 vs 1152; 2021-01: 1376 vs 1416, the PDF repeating Dec 2020; 2021-04: 1661 vs 1660).
The history table is what the dashboard publishes (`curated.fuel_price`, grade `petrol_95_coast`), so a reader of the live page sees figures that DMPR's own workbooks contradict, with no note saying so. The January 2025 case was found in CHG-2026-027;
the March 2024 case was added on Scout's report and checked here. KE-015 states the five-month fact, the two-month pattern and a pointer to this record for the rest; it does not claim a cause.
