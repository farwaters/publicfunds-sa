## Why KE-015
Open KE numbers checked 2026-10-24 on origin/main (highest KE-013) and on every remote branch: the highest anywhere is KE-014 (PR #22, `chg-2026-024-diesel-oct2022-note`); no branch uses KE-015. PR #24 (`chg-2026-026-known-gaps-ke012`) edits the KE-012 row only.

## Problem
The DMPR history PDF and DMPR's own January 2025 fuel-regulations workbook disagree on one January 2025 figure. The history table is what the dashboard publishes
(`curated.fuel_price`, grade `petrol_95_coast`), so a reader of the live page sees a figure DMPR's own workbook contradicts, with no note saying so.

## Evidence (values in c/l; read 2026-10-24, cells only, never the document properties)
Parsed with the repo parsers: `fuel_history_pdf_rows` over `data/raw/dmpr_fuel/2026-10-20/fuel-price-history-2025.pdf` (locator `...#page1-Jan`), and
`fuel_regulations_xlsx_rows` over `January-2025-Fuel-Regulations.xlsx` (DMPR `/Portals/0/Resources/Fuel Prices Adjustments/Fuel Prices Per Zone/2025/January 2025/`;
fetched 2026-10-24 ~08:50 SAST, sha256 `c85b17a1b941ad76cc31ff3076d331cded60a48553bb0d3bb24c2f37bca3b325`, 106 832 bytes; kept outside git and deleted after use).

January 2025, PDF vs xlsx (zone 9C is labelled GAUTENG in the workbook, the inland reference; 1A is the coast reference; see CHG-2026-027):

| Grade | PDF | xlsx | Equal |
|---|---|---|---|
| petrol_93_inland | 2134 | 2134 | yes |
| petrol_95_coast (95 RON lead replacement, zone 1A, cell K156) | **2055** | **2080** | **no** |
| petrol_95_inland | 2159 | 2159 | yes |
| diesel_005_inland / coast | 1928.55 / 1849.55 | 1928.55 / 1849.55 | yes |
| paraffin_inland / coast | 1326.318 / 1227.118 | 1326.318 / 1227.118 | yes |

Six of seven match. The workbook's Petrol sheet, section "93 RON UNLEADED AND LEAD REPLACEMENT", zone 1A, cell K11 is **2055**: the PDF's 95 coast figure equals the workbook's
93 coast figure. In the same workbook the 95 coast basic price is 25.0c above the 93 coast basic price (1776.7 vs 1751.7) and 2080 - 2055 = 25.

PDF coast 95 around the month (c/l): Nov 2024 2051, Dec 2024 2068, **Jan 2025 2055**, Feb 2025 2162. Dec 2024 2068 plus the official +12c = 2080 = the xlsx figure.

Official statements (both loaded and read 2026-10-24):
- SAnews, "Petrol, diesel prices to increase" (30 Dec 2024), https://www.sanews.gov.za/south-africa/petrol-diesel-prices-increase: "At the coast, a litre of 95 petrol, which cost R20.68 in December,
  will now cost R20.80 a litre from January" (Gauteng 95: R21.47 to R21.59, matching the PDF's 2147 and 2159). **This is the source of R20.80.**
- gov.za, "Minister Gwede Mantashe announces adjustment of fuel prices effective from 1 January 2025" (dated 28 Dec 2024),
  https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/minister-gwede-mantashe-announces-adjustment-fuel-prices-effective-1-january : Petrol 93 (ULP & LRP) +19.00 c/l, Petrol 95 (ULP & LRP) +12.00 c/l, and item 5, the octane
  differential changed so that the 95 and 93 retail prices differ in each fuel-pricing zone from 1 January 2025. It says the zone schedule would be published on 31 Dec 2024. It was checked for the coast figure:
  **it gives no rand prices and does not state R20.80.** It is cited for the +12c and the differential only. The +12c on Dec 2024's 2068 gives 2080, not 2055.
- oilprices.co.za also lists R20.55 for 93 coast in January 2025 (secondary, not relied on).

Conclusion: a probable slip in DMPR's PDF (the 93 coast figure in the 95 coast column). **Cause unverified; DMPR has not been asked.**
