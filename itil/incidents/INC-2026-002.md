## Impact
- `https://data.vulekamali.gov.za/api/3/action/*` fails during TLS: `SSL: UNEXPECTED_EOF_WHILE_READING`. On 19 Oct 2026 earlier it gave 500s or timeouts.
- The CKAN datastore and search can't be used.

## Mitigation / workaround
- The public dataset pages on `vulekamali.gov.za` still link resource files. We download the latest *Budgeted and Actual National Expenditure* CSV from there: 36 MB, 139,463 lines, financial years 2022–2025.
- This is the publisher's own public download link, not a workaround of access control.
